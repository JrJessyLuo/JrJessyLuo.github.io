About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at RMIT University, advised by Prof. Zhifeng Bao, Prof. J. Shane Culpepper, and Dr. Hui Luo. I am also a Visiting Researcher at The University of Queensland.

My research focuses on tabular data intelligence — building LLM-powered systems that can effectively discover, complete, and query complex tabular data at scale. I am particularly interested in the real-world challenges this entails: retrieving relevant tables from large-scale data lakes, recovering missing values from incomplete datasets, and answering complex numerical questions over semi-structured tables — all under practical cost and efficiency constraints.

I also have prior experience in AI for healthcare, developing deep learning systems for medical question answering, patient similarity search, and clinical representation learning from electronic health records.
